# Customer Guide for Modern E-commerce Platform

Welcome to our e-commerce platform! This guide will help you navigate the website, find products, make purchases, and manage your account.

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Click "Sign Up" in the top right corner of the page
2. Enter your email address and create a password
3. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox
4. Complete your profile with your name and shipping information

### Logging In

1. Click "Sign In" in the top right corner
2. Enter your email and password
3. Alternatively, use social login options (Google, Facebook, etc.)
4. Enable "Remember me" to stay logged in on your device

## Browsing Products

### Homepage Navigation

The homepage features:
- Featured products carousel
- Special offers and promotions
- Category highlights
- Brand showcase
- Newsletter signup

### Product Categories

1. Click "Categories" in the main navigation
2. Browse all available categories
3. Click on a category to view related products
4. Use subcategories to narrow your search

### Product Listings

Product listing pages show:
- Product images
- Product names
- Prices (with discounts if applicable)
- Average ratings
- Quick add to cart buttons

### Product Details

Click on any product to view detailed information:
- Multiple product images
- Detailed description
- Features and specifications
- Customer reviews
- Related products
- Available variants (size, color, etc.)
- Stock availability

## Search and Filtering

### Using the Search Bar

1. Click the search icon or bar in the header
2. Type keywords related to the product you're looking for
3. Results will update as you type
4. Press Enter to view full search results

### Visual Search

1. Click the camera icon in the search bar
2. Upload an image or take a photo
3. The system will find products similar to your image

### Filtering Options

On category and search result pages:
1. Use the sidebar filters to narrow results
2. Filter by:
   - Price range
   - Category
   - Brand
   - Rating
   - Other attributes specific to the product type
3. Sort products by:
   - Featured
   - Price: Low to High
   - Price: High to Low
   - Newest
   - Best Selling
   - Highest Rated

## Shopping Cart

### Adding Items

1. Click "Add to Cart" on the product page
2. Adjust quantity using the + and - buttons
3. Select any required options (size, color, etc.)
4. A notification will confirm when an item is added

### Viewing Your Cart

1. Click the cart icon in the header
2. Review items in your cart
3. Adjust quantities or remove items
4. See subtotal, estimated tax, and shipping

### Saving Items for Later

1. Click "Save for Later" on any cart item
2. Items will be moved to your wishlist
3. Access your wishlist from your account page

## Checkout Process

### Starting Checkout

1. Review your cart
2. Click "Proceed to Checkout"
3. Log in if you haven't already

### Shipping Information

1. Enter your shipping address or select a saved address
2. Choose a shipping method (Standard, Express, etc.)
3. Add special delivery instructions if needed

### Payment

1. Enter payment details or select a saved payment method
2. Review order summary
3. Apply any discount codes
4. Click "Place Order" to complete your purchase

### Order Confirmation

After successful payment:
1. You'll see an order confirmation page
2. A confirmation email will be sent to your email address
3. Track your order status from your account page

## Managing Your Account

### Account Dashboard

Access your account dashboard by clicking your name in the header. From here you can:
- View order history
- Manage shipping addresses
- Update payment methods
- Edit your profile information
- Access your wishlist

### Order History

1. Go to "Order History" in your account dashboard
2. View all past orders
3. Click on any order to see details
4. Track shipments
5. Request returns if needed

### Address Book

1. Go to "Addresses" in your account dashboard
2. Add, edit, or delete shipping addresses
3. Set a default shipping address

### Payment Methods

1. Go to "Payment Methods" in your account dashboard
2. Add, edit, or delete payment methods
3. Set a default payment method

## Returns and Refunds

### Return Policy

Our return policy allows:
- Returns within 30 days of delivery
- Items must be in original condition with tags
- Some items may not be eligible for return (noted on product page)

### Initiating a Return

1. Go to "Order History" in your account dashboard
2. Find the order containing the item to return
3. Click "Return Items"
4. Select the items to return and reason for return
5. Follow the instructions for packaging and shipping
6. Print the return label

### Refund Process

After we receive your return:
1. We'll inspect the item
2. You'll receive an email when your refund is processed
3. Refunds typically take 5-10 business days to appear on your payment method
